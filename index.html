📅 Q3 Catalysts — Price Tests & Clearance
🏷️ Q3 Price Tests — market-adjusted · SJS Jun 29 · HNS 60ct + MDC Jul 12 · Sleepzyme Aug 2 · EHE Aug 16
Four price tests (SJS, MDC, Sleepzyme, EHE) plus one clearance (HNS 60ct — discontinuing, ~9,500 units to move). Every read below is market-adjusted: each SKU's raw change is divided by the drift of a control cohort measured over that SKU's own baseline and test windows. Volume multiplier = units needed to hold gross profit at the lower price (from the pricing models); where no pricing model exists the revenue-neutral multiple is shown and labelled.
📐 Control cohort & method. Control = Rub On Relief 1/2/3-Pack + Natural Colon Cleanse — the SKUs that ran no price action in any window and share no bundle with a test SKU. The HNS 90ct family is deliberately excluded from the control because the 60ct clearance may act on it. Drift is measured per test, over that test's exact windows, so no single blanket factor is applied: SJS (Jun 1–22 → Jul 29–Aug 11) +0.2% units / +1.6% rev · HNS 60ct & MDC (Jul 1–11 → Jul 12–Aug 11) −15.0% / −12.7% · Sleepzyme (Jul 1–Aug 1 → Aug 2–11) +3.7% / +3.3%. This is why a single factor would mislead: the market was flat across the SJS windows but down 15% across the 60ct/MDC windows. Applying the account-wide −10.27%/−13.26% everywhere would have wrongly rescued SJS.
📅 Two price changes land Aug 16 (3 days out). Sleepzyme steps $9.95 → $14.95. Eye Health Essentials launches at $11.99 (from $27.00) for two weeks, then $18.99 for four weeks from ~Aug 30. Both fall inside August, so MTD will blend pre- and post-change pricing.
